Frequently Asked Questions

How are monthly calls counted?
Every successful HTTP POST to a `/api/v1/*` endpoint or MCP `tools/call` execution decrements your monthly quota by 1. Preflight OPTIONS and schema inspection queries do not consume quota.
Do you store any of my computational inputs?
No. TrueCalci guarantees zero persistent storage for computational payloads. All statutory algorithms run inside transient Cloudflare V8 memory isolates and are wiped immediately.
Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept global Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. For Indian developers, we natively support UPI AutoPay and RuPay via Dodo Payments (Merchant of Record).
Can I cancel or upgrade at any time?
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. You can cancel with a single click from your Subscriptions dashboard, and access continues until the end of your billing cycle.
