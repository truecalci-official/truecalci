TrueCalci Deterministic Compute Documentation

High-precision deterministic calculation engine for AI agents, fintech engineering teams, and corporate tax analysts. TrueCalci eliminates large language model arithmetic hallucinations through formal statutory codification and closed-form analytical equations.

1. Deterministic vs Probabilistic Compute

Generative AI models and LLMs (such as Claude, GPT-4, and Gemini) predict statistical distributions of tokens. While exceptional at natural language synthesis, probabilistic models inherently suffer from arithmetic drift, rounding inaccuracies, and regulatory hallucinations when calculating tax brackets, compound amortization, and options Greeks.

TrueCalci provides an external deterministic verification oracle. Every calculation is executed in sub-millisecond Cloudflare V8 edge isolates or client-side WebAssembly using IEEE 754-2019 double-precision floats and exact statutory bracket lookups.

2. Codified Statutory Authorities & Rules

All 24 computational engines hosted on TrueCalci derive directly from published government legislation, tax treaties, and mathematical consensus standards:

IRC §1402 & §199A — Remote Contractor Parity & QBI Official IRS Pub 535 ↗

Self-Employment Contributions Act (SECA) imposes 15.3% tax (12.4% OASDI up to the statutory cap $168,600, plus 2.9% Medicare without ceiling). Qualified Business Income (QBI) deduction under Section 199A provides an above-the-line deduction of up to 20% of net eligible self-employment income, subject to phase-out limits.

IRS Revenue Ruling 74-288 — S-Corp Reasonable Compensation IRS S-Corp Compensation Guidance ↗

Requires shareholder-employees of S-Corporations to take reasonable W-2 wages before distributing remaining profits as non-FICA dividend distributions. TrueCalci computes exact FICA tax arbitrage and compliance safety margins.

IRC §415(c) & Notice 2023-75 — Solo 401(k) Contribution Limits IRS One-Participant 401(k) Guidance ↗

Calculates dual-tier retirement contributions: employee elective deferral (up to $23,000 / $30,500 catch-up) plus employer profit sharing (25% of net Schedule C adjusted profit), subject to the $69,000 total annual ceiling.

Income-tax Act, 2025 & Finance Act — Section 115BAC Slabs Income Tax Department Official Slabs ↗

Enforces New Concessional Tax Regime rates for Tax Year 2026-27: ₹75,000 standard deduction, revised 5% to 30% slabs, Section 87A marginal rebate for taxable incomes up to ₹7,00,000, and 4% Health and Education Cess.

CFPB Reg Z (12 CFR § 1026) & Homeowners Protection Act 1998 CFPB Regulation Z Authority ↗

Calculates monthly Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance (PITI) with statutory Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) cancellation triggers: automatic termination when principal reaches 78% original purchase price LTV.

Council Directive 2006/112/EC — European VAT Rules EUR-Lex European Law Directive ↗

Calculates inclusive and exclusive VAT derivations across all 27 European Union member states and reverse charge cross-border B2B digital services rules.
