Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act & Global Privacy Standards

🔒 Your Financial Data Never Leaves Your Device: All calculations on OmniCalc India (Income Tax, Mutual Funds SIP, Home Loan EMI, Gold Billing, PPF, and Engineering Formulas) execute 100% client-side in your local web browser. We do not store, log, transmit, or sell your personal income, loans, or investment data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike traditional financial portals, OmniCalc does not require user accounts, logins, phone numbers, or email sign-ups to access any calculator. We never collect or store:

  • Your salary, CTC, tax deductions, or PAN details.
  • Your loan amounts, bank account balances, or credit scores.
  • Your gold purchases, jewellery bills, or property dimensions.
  • Personal identification information (PII).

2. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) & Aggregated Traffic Data

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to understand aggregated website performance, popular calculator tools (e.g., number of visitors using the Income Tax vs. SIP calculator), device types, and browser compatibility.

Google Analytics uses standard cookies to collect anonymized telemetry such as page views, session duration, and approximate geographic location (city/country level). IP anonymization is active by default. You can prevent Google Analytics from tracking your visits by using browser ad/tracker blockers or installing the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Local Storage (Browser Preferences)

We use standard browser localStorage solely to save your local UI preferences:

  • calc_theme: Remembers your Light or Dark Mode visual preference.
  • calc_history: Optionally saves recent calculations locally on your device for your convenience.

This data remains strictly stored on your personal device and is never transmitted to external servers. You can clear this anytime by clearing your browser cache.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

To keep OmniCalc free for all students, taxpayers, and households, we may display unobtrusive advertisements served by third-party advertising partners such as Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. External Links

OmniCalc may contain reference links to official government portals (such as the Income Tax Department e-filing portal at incometax.gov.in, SEBI, or RBI). We are not responsible for the privacy policies or practices of external third-party websites.

6. Updates & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new legal regulations or features. If you have questions regarding this policy or data privacy on OmniCalc, you can reach out via our official GitHub repository or webmaster contact.
