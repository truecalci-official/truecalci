Privacy Policy & Trust Center

Effective Date: September 2026 | Compliant with Global Privacy Standards & Zero-Storage Guarantees

🔒 Your Financial Data Never Leaves Your Device: All calculations on TrueCalci execute 100% client-side in your local web browser or in transient Cloudflare V8 edge isolates. TrueCalci maintains a strict zero-storage architecture: we never store, log, transmit, or monetize your salaries, property values, or portfolio inputs.

1. Information We Do Not Collect (Zero Retention Policy)

TrueCalci operates on a strict Zero Calculation Data Retention architecture. We never collect, store, log, or persist:

  • Your salaries, contractor billings, revenue figures, or tax bracket inputs.
  • Your prompt tokens, completion tokens, or AI model optimization payloads.
  • Your mortgage balances, bank credentials, or credit figures.
  • Your polynomial coefficients, engineering matrices, or mathematical formulas.

All numerical calculations execute strictly in volatile, transient memory (ephemeral client-side JavaScript or stateless Cloudflare Worker V8 isolates). The moment your JSON calculation output is rendered, the data ceases to exist on our infrastructure.

2. Information Collected Strictly for Account & API Operations

We only collect and store the minimum data strictly necessary to maintain your developer account and enforce fair usage:

  • Authentication Identity: Your email address and account ID (retrieved via Google or GitHub OAuth SSO) to authenticate your access.
  • API Rate Limiting & Token Counters: Ephemeral token buckets and request timestamps associated with your API key to enforce rate limits and track monthly request quotas.

3. Account Deletion & Statutory Data Retention Schedule

You have the absolute right to delete your TrueCalci account at any time. Our data lifecycle upon deletion follows strict international privacy laws (GDPR Art. 17, CCPA, India DPDP Act 2023):

  • Immediate Revocation: Your API keys, authentication sessions, and quota counters are immediately revoked and permanently deleted from active edge caches within 30 days.
  • Statutory Tax & Financial Records: As required by international tax and anti-money laundering laws (e.g., US IRS Code § 6001, UK HMRC regulations, EU VAT Directive, Indian Income Tax Act 1961 § 44AA), historical payment transactions and tax invoices processed via our Merchant of Record (Dodo Payments) are retained for the statutory period of 7 years solely for legal, audit, and tax compliance.

4. Local Storage (Browser Preferences)

We use standard browser localStorage solely on your device to save your local UI preferences without transmitting them to our servers:

  • calc_theme: Remembers your Light or Dark Mode visual appearance preference.
  • calc_region: Remembers your selected currency display standard (USD $ / INR ₹).

5. Zero Third-Party Advertising Policy (No Google AdSense)

TrueCalci is a premium, distraction-free enterprise platform supported by developer API subscriptions. We do not display third-party ad networks (such as Google AdSense), ad trackers, or sponsor banners. Your calculation workflow is 100% ad-free.

5. Authorized Third-Party Service Providers

TrueCalci partners with industry-leading third-party infrastructure providers to deliver secure edge compute, billing, and authentication:

  • Merchant of Record & Payment Rails: Commercial developer subscriptions and metered usage payments are processed securely by Dodo Payments Inc. ("Dodo Payments") as our authorized Merchant of Record. Dodo Payments handles PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant card processing, UPI AutoPay, currency settlement, and automated GST/VAT tax invoicing. TrueCalci never accesses or stores your raw card numbers or CVVs.
  • Edge Compute & Security Network: TrueCalci runs across global edge locations powered by Cloudflare Inc. Cloudflare provides DDoS protection, SSL/TLS certificate termination, HTTP routing, and transient V8 isolate execution.
  • OAuth 2.0 Authentication: Developer authentication is facilitated via official single-sign-on (SSO) APIs provided by GitHub Inc. and Google LLC. We only retrieve your public name, email, and handle to establish your developer API access tier.
  • Performance Telemetry: Aggregated, privacy-shielded system performance analytics are monitored via Google Analytics 4 (GA4) with IP anonymization active.

6. External Links & Statutory Calculators

TrueCalci may contain reference links to official government and regulatory resources (such as the IRS, SEBI, HMRC, or RBI). We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or cookie policies of external third-party websites.

7. Policy Amendments & Inquiries

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new legal regulations or platform capabilities. If you have questions regarding data protection on TrueCalci, you can reach out via our official GitHub repository or webmaster contact.
